## About

The Fedora Week of Diversity (FWD) is an event dedicated to celebrating individuality in the Fedora community, and how it keeps the community standing strong on pillars of mutual respect, tolerance and encouragement.

Six months after presenting the Fedora Badges revamp at the F39 Release Party, I was back on the release-party stage. At the **Fedora Linux 40 Release Party**, Emma Kidney and I presented the DEI Team's contributions to community enrichment, and promoted the upcoming Fedora Week of Diversity 2024.
