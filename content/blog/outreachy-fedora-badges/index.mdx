## Fedora Badges Design

The [Fedora Project](https://fedoraproject.org/), a global online community founded in 2003 through a Red Hat partnership, is dedicated to enhancing lives through free software and fosters a vibrant open source ecosystem with tens of thousands of contributors. As part of this initiative, Fedora Badges was created to recognise contributors, facilitate engagement for new and existing members, and drive infrastructure improvements.

During the Outreachy May to August 2023 internship cohort, I was selected from over 3,600 applicants to contribute to the [Fedora Badges Design](https://badges.fedoraproject.org/about) project. This experience gave me valuable hands-on skills in design software, illustration and design theory, all under excellent mentorship.

## Our Target 🎯

**Enhancing contributor experience through a badges revamp.**

The Fedora Badges revamp is a strategic initiative designed to substantially increase contributor engagement and satisfaction. By implementing a more rewarding and prestigious badge system, we motivate contributors to actively participate and take pride in their earned achievements.

Beyond increased contributions, this initiative improves contributor retention, fosters a stronger sense of community ownership, and provides clear pathways for contributor growth and recognition within the Fedora Project.

## Workflow & Execution

Throughout my internship, I followed a structured workflow built on consistent communication and collaborative feedback. This included weekly stand-up meetings to align on objectives, in-chat review and feedback sessions for real-time iteration, and careful documentation of progress and feedback in a shared spreadsheet.

This approach ensured transparency, efficient project execution, and continuous improvement.

## Project Reveal & Wrap-up

On Day 2 of the Fedora [F39 Release Party](https://events.ringcentral.com/events/fedora-linux-39-release-party/registration), my co-intern and I presented the work we had done so far on the **Fedora Badges Design** Outreachy mentored project. It was my second time speaking to a large part of the Fedora community, and this time I was able to shake off all the fright that comes with public speaking 😓. I consider myself a better speaker now.

Our presentation showcased the revamped designs and gave the community a walkthrough of the "behind the scenes" of our design work, highlighting the thought process behind our design choices 🎨
